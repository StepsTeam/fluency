# Passage Page
*(Market Opportunity – TAM / SAM / SOM, Traction & Roadmap)*

## Passage Overview
Security operations centers worldwide spend billions annually on SIEM platforms, SOAR tools, and security analytics to manage exploding alert volumes and sophisticated threats. The shift toward AI-driven, autonomous SOC operations creates a massive opportunity for platforms like Fluency that combine behavioral analytics with real-time automation.

## 1. Market Opportunity

**Market Definition**  
Global SIEM and security analytics software market, specifically the modern/next-generation SIEM segment focused on behavioral analytics and autonomous SOC operations.

**Customer Segments**  
- SMB security teams (50-500 employees)  
- Mid-market SOCs (500-5,000 employees)  
- Enterprise security operations (5,000+ employees)  
- Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs) serving multiple clients

**Problem & Spend Today**  
Organizations currently spend $50K-$5M+ annually on SIEM licensing, security analytics, and SOC operations staffing. The average enterprise SOC spends 4-6 hours per analyst daily on alert triage alone, creating massive inefficiency.

## 2. Market Size – TAM / SAM / SOM

**Total Addressable Market (TAM)**  
- Definition: All organizations worldwide using SIEM/security analytics solutions  
- Inputs: ~250,000 organizations globally × $150K average annual spend  
- TAM Formula: 250K customers × $150K = $37.5B/year  
- **TAM Estimate: ≈ $38B/year**

**Serviceable Available Market (SAM)**  
- Definition: North America/Europe organizations seeking modern SIEM/autonomous SOC  
- Inputs: ~50,000 mid-market/enterprise orgs + 2,000 MSSPs × $120K avg spend  
- SAM Formula: 52K customers × $120K = $6.2B/year  
- **SAM Estimate: ≈ $6B/year**

**Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**  
- Definition: Realistic share Fluency can capture in 3-5 years  
- Target Share: 2% of SAM through direct sales + MSSP partnerships  
- SOM Formula: $6B × 2% = $120M ARR  
- **SOM Estimate: ≈ $120M ARR in 3-5 years**

**Key Assumptions**  
1. Modern SIEM/autonomous SOC grows from 15% to 35% market share by 2028  
2. Average ACV remains $100K-$150K for target customer segments  
3. MSSP channel represents 30-40% of total addressable opportunity
