# Profess Page

*(Academic Research & Evidence)*



## Profess Overview

Academic and practitioner research supports the case for Fluency by showing that behavioral analytics, UEBA, and AI-assisted SOC operations improve early threat detection, reduce alert fatigue, and help analysts focus on the most meaningful activity. The evidence base suggests that behavior-centered security is especially valuable for catching novel attacks, insider threats, and low-and-slow intrusions that signature-based systems often miss.



## Core Research Papers

### Primary Paper

**Enberg, P. (2024). *Behavior Analytics in Cyber Security*. Theseus.**

**Key Insight:**<br>
Behavior analytics improves cybersecurity by using AI and machine learning to identify abnormal activity patterns that may indicate insider threats or novel attacks.

**Demonstrated Impact:**<br>
The paper supports the claim that behavior analytics strengthens early threat detection and helps security teams identify suspicious activity that traditional controls may overlook.

**Relevance to Our Product:**<br>
This validates Fluency’s core approach of using behavioral analysis and UEBA-style detection to surface meaningful security events faster and with more context.



### Secondary Paper

**CEUR-WS.org (2025). *The Role of Artificial Intelligence in SOC Operations*.**

**Key Insight:**<br>
SOC practitioners report strong interest in AI-driven automation because it helps solve alert fatigue, information overload, weak triage, and false-positive filtering.

**Demonstrated Impact:**<br>
The study found broad practitioner interest in AI for incident prioritization, anomaly detection, and reducing false positives.

**Relevance to Our Product:**<br>
This supports Fluency’s autonomous SOC positioning by showing that AI-based triage and prioritization address real operational pain points in security teams.



### Supporting Paper

**Arxiv (2025). *A Unified Framework for Human–AI Collaboration in Security Operations Centers*.**

**Key Insight:**<br>
Human-AI collaboration in SOCs is most effective when AI autonomy is paired with trust calibration and structured human oversight.

**Demonstrated Impact:**<br>
The framework highlights reduced alert fatigue and improved response coordination through calibrated collaboration between humans and AI.

**Relevance to Our Product:**<br>
This supports Fluency’s model of autonomous workflows with analyst review, reinforcing the idea that automation works best when it is explainable and operationally controlled.



## Synthesis & Positioning (MOST CRITICAL SECTION)

**What the Literature Proves**<br>
Behavioral analytics and AI-assisted SOC workflows can improve threat detection, reduce false positives, and lower analyst workload in environments where alert volume and attack complexity are high.

**Why This Matters for Us**<br>
This evidence derisks Fluency’s core thesis by showing that the market pain is real and that behavior-based detection plus AI automation are credible ways to solve it. It also supports the timing of the product, since organizations are actively looking for ways to reduce operational overload without losing security coverage.

**Our Distinct Angle vs Prior Work**<br>
Fluency goes beyond research by productizing behavioral analytics into a real-time, autonomous SOC platform that is designed for operational use, partner deployment, and continuous workflow execution rather than academic demonstration.
