# Promise Page

## 1. Core Promise
- **Value Proposition Statement:**  
  Fluency gives security teams real-time behavioral threat detection and autonomous SOC workflows so they can focus on meaningful threats instead of alert overload.

## 2. Customer Benefit (WIIFM – What's In It For Me)

- **Functional Benefits:**  
  Fluency turns raw security data into behavior-aware cases, automates investigation workflows, and accelerates response times without replacing existing tools.

- **Emotional Benefits:**  
  Security teams gain confidence knowing they're catching real threats while spending less time on false positives and routine triage.

- **Financial / Practical Gains:**  
  Reduced analyst fatigue, faster mean-time-to-analyze, and lower operational costs through automation of repetitive SOC workflows.

## 3. Differentiation

- **Unique Advantage / Edge:**  
  Fluency combines streaming behavioral analytics, UEBA clustering, and autonomous AI workflows into one platform designed specifically for real-time SOC operations.

- **Proof Points:**  
  Processes 6.5 petabytes yearly across 10 AWS regions, founded by experienced security leaders, positioned as "AI SIEM for the Autonomous SOC."

## 4. Existing Solutions & Gaps

- **Current Alternatives:**  
  Traditional SIEM platforms (Splunk, QRadar), cloud-native SIEMs (Microsoft Sentinel), SOAR tools, and endpoint security platforms expanding into SIEM.

- **Limitations or Frustrations with Current Solutions:**  
  Legacy SIEMs create alert fatigue through retrospective analysis; modern platforms often lack deep behavioral understanding or autonomous workflows; customers face high costs and complexity replacing entrenched tooling.

## 5. Brand Promise Summary

- **Concise Positioning Statement:**  
  "For overwhelmed security teams, Fluency delivers behavior-aware threat detection and autonomous SOC operations — turning alert chaos into confident security outcomes."

- **Customer Transformation:**  
  From buried in noisy alerts → to focused on real threats with automated context and workflows.

## 6. Strategic Relevance

- **Business Impact Connection:**  
  This promise positions Fluency as the modern security operations layer that works with existing tools, enabling both direct sales to SOC teams and partner-led deployments through MSSPs and cybersecurity service providers.

---

**Why This Solution Is Needed Now:**  
Security teams face exploding data volumes, sophisticated threats, and staffing shortages. Behavioral analytics and autonomous workflows address these pressures by catching novel attacks signature-based systems miss while reducing analyst burnout through intelligent automation.
