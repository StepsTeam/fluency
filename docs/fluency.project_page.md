# Project Page: Fluency Security (Text-Friendly Markdown)

## Financial Plan & Market Timing

Fluency Security’s public site positions the company as an AI SIEM and autonomous SOC platform with behavioral analytics, UEBA clustering, gap detection, AI-driven workflows, and a “Talk to Sales” motion. It does not publish a financial model.

Based on its pricing and positioning, the most reasonable model is a usage-based security SaaS business serving enterprise and MSP customers, where revenue scales with users/assets and data volume rather than one-time licenses.

---

## Financial Overview

- **Projection period:** 5 years<br>
- **Revenue growth:** Rapid early expansion driven by pilots and MSP/channel adoption, followed by moderation as the customer base grows<br>
- **Expense forecast:** Heavy early R&D and go-to-market spend, with slower relative growth in G&A over time<br>
- **Burn rate:** High in years 1–2, declining as gross profit and recurring revenue expand<br>
- **Runway:** Assumes early-stage capital is used to reach repeatable revenue before the next major raise<br>

## Revenue Model

Fluency’s pricing suggests two metered billing paths:

- **Asset-based pricing:**<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- ~$10 per user<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- ~$16 per server<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Minimum monthly charge<br>

- **Capacity-based pricing:**<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- ~$2.99 per GB<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Minimum monthly charge<br>

This structure supports a recurring ARR model with strong expansion potential as customer environments grow.

### Customer Segments

- **SMB / Mid-market**<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- 25–100 endpoints/users<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Lower ACV<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Faster sales cycles<br>

- **MSSP / Channel**<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Multi-tenant contracts<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Higher ACV<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Strong retention<br>

- **Enterprise**<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Large data and asset volumes<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Highest ACV<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Slower sales cycles but strong expansion<br>

## Expense Forecast

- **R&D**<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Highest in years 1–2<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Driven by AI/ML, behavioral analytics, automation, and product hardening<br>

- **Sales & Marketing**<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Moderate-to-high initially<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Becomes more efficient with partner-led and referral growth<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Consultative “Talk to Sales” model<br>

- **G&A**<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Slower growth than revenue<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Includes compliance, legal, finance, and admin<br>

- **Hosting / COGS**<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Scales with data volume<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Margins improve with platform efficiency and automation<br>
