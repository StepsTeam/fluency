## Company Positioning Notes

Fluency describes itself as an AI SIEM and autonomous SOC platform built on behavioral analytics, UEBA clustering, and gap detection.  
The company emphasizes faster investigation, lower operational cost, context-rich alerts, and seamless integration with existing tools and workflows.  
Its About Us page says it was launched in 2013, operates across 10 AWS regions, and processes 6.5 petabytes yearly.  
Fluency also states that it is based in Rockville, Maryland, and that the development team works locally in the area.
